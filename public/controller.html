📱 PolyZone Controller

On the PC, press Connect phone (or open the menu with Esc) and enter the 8-character code shown, or just scan the QR code. Your phone and PC must be on the same Wi-Fi, or use the online address.

    Left side: movement joystick (push all the way up to sprint) · drag on the right: look around · hold FIRE to shoot · JUMP: jump / vault over cover · CROUCH: crouch (slide while sprinting) · ⚙ Layout: drag buttons and the joystick where you want them, resize them, pick a Floating or Fixed joystick — saved on this phone
